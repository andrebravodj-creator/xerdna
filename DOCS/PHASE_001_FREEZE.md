# PHASE 001 FREEZE

**Prepared by:** the independent XERDNA Scientific Architecture Review Board, on the authority of `DOCS/PHASE_001_APPROVAL_V3.md` (APPROVED).
**Status:** Permanent governance record. This document freezes the constitutional and architectural foundation of Phase 1 — Universal Biological Memory — as the baseline every future phase inherits from and is measured against. It does not modify any other document. It does not authorize implementation by itself — see `DOCS/IMPLEMENTATION_POLICY.md`, its companion, for how implementation may now proceed without violating what is frozen here.

**What "freeze" means in this context:** the documents named below stop being *live drafts* and become *baseline references*. They can still be amended — VISION.md, MASTER_CONTEXT.md, and ROADMAP.md via the Article III process; GRAPH/SCHEMA.md via its own changelog discipline — but any future change to a frozen document is now a change *against a known, dated baseline*, not an edit to a document still being actively shaped. This is the difference between "this is what we currently think" and "this is what everything after this point is built on."

## Mission

To organize humanity's biological knowledge and accelerate scientific discovery through artificial intelligence — while never replacing laboratory validation, never claiming discoveries, and never blurring the line between established evidence, computational prediction, and research hypothesis. ## Rules That Future Phases Cannot Violate

Frozen, inviolable, binding on every phase from Phase 2 onward without exception (ROADMAP.md Article I: "every phase inherits the full constitution"):

1. **Three-tier evidence model, always** (MASTER_CONTEXT.md Article II Rule 1; VISION.md Article IV). Every biological claim is exactly one of Established Evidence, Computational Prediction, or Research Hypothesis. Untiered data does not enter the system.
2. **Never claim discoveries** (Article II Rule 2; VISION.md Article V Rule 2).
3. **Assist, never replace** (Article II Rule 3; VISION.md Article VI Rule 1). No laboratory validation, peer review, or scientific judgment is ever substituted for.
4. **Build for 2,000 phases, not the next 20** (Article II Rule 4; VISION.md Article IX) — see below.
5. **Scientific Constitution Before Code** (Article II Rule 5; VISION.md Article IX) — see below.
6. **Biosecurity and ethics are absolute** (Article II Rule 6; VISION.md Article VII). No commercial, technical, or competitive consideration ever outranks them.
7. **No autonomous claims by AI components** (VISION.md Article VI Rule 2) — every AI output carries one of the three evidence tiers, none of them "certainty."
8. **Method transparency and reproducibility** (VISION.md Article VI Rule 3, Article V Rule 4) — every prediction names its method and version; every finding traces to its source data.
9. **Human-in-the-loop by design** (VISION.md Article VI Rule 5) — a human or an experiment is always the final arbiter of truth, at every phase including Phase 5 (Autonomous Scientist) and Phase 6 (Collective Intelligence).
10. **Refutation is signal, not noise** (VISION.md Article V Rule 3) — disproven hypotheses and predictions are recorded, not erased.

## Governance Pipeline

**Constitution → Architecture → Review → Decision → Audit → Remediation → Approval → Implementation → Validation → Release** (MASTER_CONTEXT.md Article XII, ratified v1.1). No stage may be skipped; no phase may enter Implementation while any blocking finding from its own Audit stage remains unresolved. This pipeline itself is now part of the frozen baseline — Phase 2 and every phase after it is bound by it, not just Phase 1.

## Scientific Constitution Before Code

No implementation — schema, database, ingestion pipeline, API, backend service, or interface — is authoritative until it is traceable to a ratified article in the constitutional documents (VISION.md Article IX; MASTER_CONTEXT.md Article II Rule 5). This freeze is the concrete demonstration of the principle, not an exception to it: Phase 1's architecture is frozen precisely because it has now been traced, reviewed, decided, audited, remediated, and approved — in that order, with nothing skipped.

## Build for 2,000 Phases, Not the Next 20

Architectural decisions on load-bearing components — the knowledge graph, the evidence model, the provenance layer, entity identity — are made as if hundreds of future phases will be built on top of them by people not currently in the room (VISION.md Article IX; MASTER_CONTEXT.md Article II Rule 4, Article IV). Every DEFER item in GRAPH/SCHEMA_DECISIONS.md, every explicitly-unpinned ontology (ChEBI, SO), and every open backlog item in DOCS/ARCHITECTURE_AUDIT_V2.md is a deliberate application of this rule, not an oversight — recorded as open rather than silently resolved, so it survives past this session's memory.
