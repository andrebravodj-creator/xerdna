# ROADMAP

**This is a constitutional document — the Phase Structure of XERDNA.** It sequences the mission ([VISION.md](VISION.md)) under the governance and authority hierarchy defined in [MASTER_CONTEXT.md](MASTER_CONTEXT.md). It describes a decades-long trajectory, not a sprint plan.

## Article I — How to Read This Roadmap

- **No dates.** XERDNA is built for 2,000 phases, not the next 20 (VISION.md Article IX) — assigning quarters or years to phases this early would manufacture false precision. Phases advance when their constitutional and architectural foundation is solid, not when a calendar says so.
- **Phases overlap.** Phase 2 (reasoning) cannot mature without Phase 1 (the graph) already growing, and Phase 1 never actually "finishes" — it keeps expanding underneath every later phase. Treat phase boundaries as centers of gravity, not hard gates.
- **Every phase inherits the full constitution.** The Evidence Principles, AI Principles, Ethical Principles, and Non-Negotiable Rules (VISION.md, MASTER_CONTEXT.md) are not things later phases add — they hold from Phase 1 onward, without exception.
- **Phase advancement is a governed act, not an announcement.** A phase is considered "reached" only once its technical designs trace back to ratified constitutional articles (MASTER_CONTEXT.md Article I, Article II Rule 5) — not merely once someone decides to start building.
- **Current status: Phase 1, foundational stage.** The repository currently contains constitutional and structural documentation only — no application code, schema, or implementation has been authorized yet.

---

## Article II — Phase 1 — Universal Biological Memory

**Objective:** Build the world's largest biological knowledge graph.

Connect, as first-class entities with provenance:

- Genomes, genes, DNA, RNA, proteins
- Cell types and biological pathways
- Evolution and protein structures
- Mutations
- Drugs and clinical trials
- Patents
- Microbiomes
- Public biological databases
- Scientific literature

Everything becomes one living knowledge graph. This phase establishes the entity model, the evidence-tier data model, and the ingestion approach that every later phase depends on — it is the foundation the other 1,999+ phases are built on top of.

---

## Article III — Phase 2 — Biological Reasoning Engine

**Objective:** Move beyond search into reasoning over the graph built in Phase 1.

Find:

- Hidden relationships between entities that are not explicitly linked
- Missing links in the graph
- Conflicting evidence between sources
- Research gaps — areas with surprisingly little evidence
- Unexplored areas of the biological space

The output of this phase is structured findings over the graph, always labeled by evidence tier — never new "facts" asserted outright.

---

## Article IV — Phase 3 — Hypothesis Engine

**Objective:** Generate evidence-ranked scientific hypotheses from the reasoning engine's findings.

Every hypothesis must include:

- Supporting evidence
- Contradicting evidence
- Confidence level
- Scientific references
- An explanation of the reasoning that produced it

**Hard constraint:** XERDNA never claims discoveries. It generates hypotheses only, for researchers to pursue or refute.

---

## Article V — Phase 4 — Biological Simulator

**Objective:** Create a digital biological universe to visualize what the graph and reasoning engine describe.

Visualize:

- DNA and RNA
- Proteins and organelles
- Signaling
- The immune system
- Cellular processes

**Hard constraint:** every visualization must clearly and persistently distinguish established biology from simulation output. A simulation is a Computational Prediction, rendered — it is never presented with the visual authority of measured fact.

---

## Article VI — Phase 5 — Autonomous Scientist

**Objective:** Give the platform an ongoing, self-driven research loop.

Continuously:

- Read scientific literature
- Update the knowledge graph
- Detect patterns across the graph
- Propose new research questions
- Rank promising hypotheses

This phase turns Phases 1–3 from a system researchers query into a system that keeps working between queries. Per VISION.md Article VI, autonomy here applies to research effort, never to validation — a human or an experiment remains the final arbiter of truth.

---

## Article VII — Phase 6 — Collective Intelligence

**Objective:** Open the loop to the research community.

Researchers contribute:

- Hypotheses
- Experiments
- Validations
- Refutations

The biological knowledge graph continuously evolves from both the Autonomous Scientist (Phase 5) and human contribution. Refutation is treated as seriously as confirmation — a disproven hypothesis is valuable signal, not noise (VISION.md Article V).

---

## Article VIII — Phase 7 — Biological Operating System

**Objective:** Become the intelligence layer for biology, not a destination researchers visit but infrastructure others build on.

Serves:

- Universities
- Hospitals
- Biotech
- Pharmaceutical companies
- Diagnostics
- Academia

Everyone builds on the same biological intelligence layer instead of re-deriving their own. Per VISION.md Article VII, this phase favors broad access over gatekeeping.

---

## Article IX — Phase 8 — Discovery Network

**Objective:** Shift the goal from answering questions to surfacing questions humanity doesn't yet know to ask.

The goal is not answering questions. The goal is helping humanity discover what it does not yet know.

---

## Article X — Beyond Phase 8

Phase 8 is the current horizon of vision, not the end of XERDNA (VISION.md Article III, IX). Phases beyond it are not yet named because naming them now would manufacture false precision — they are added only through the constitutional amendment process (MASTER_CONTEXT.md Article III), each new phase justified against the mission in VISION.md before it is written into this document.

## Article XI — What This Roadmap Is Not

- It is not a fixed deadline schedule.
- It is not a complete backlog — each phase decomposes into its own detailed, constitutionally-traceable design documents when it is actively underway.
- It is not the ceiling. "Phase 8" is the current horizon of vision, not the end of XERDNA. See VISION.md Article IX — *build for 2,000 phases, not the next 20.*
